# Jupiter Fact Sheet

## Overview

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and a gas giant made mostly of
hydrogen and helium. Its Great Red Spot is a giant storm larger than Earth.

## Magnetosphere

Jupiter has the strongest magnetic field of any planet in the Solar System.
